Who we are

Hope starts with people, opportunity and action.

We, the members of Hope Builders Initiative-Uganda, commit to empowering women and protecting children's rights, while promoting climate resilience and sustainable development.

RHUL is a legally registered Community Based Organization (CBO) under the laws of Uganda. Our work is rooted in communities, with a focus on practical solutions that strengthen families, expand opportunity and help communities prepare for a changing climate.

Why Donate To Us? • Trust & Accountability

Responsible structures. Community ownership. Sustainable action.

From the supplied organizational information, donors should be able to see how RHUL is governed and how accountability is approached.

Governed Structure

We have a General Assembly (supreme body), Board of Directors, and an Executive Director serving a 3-year renewable term.

Financial Accountability

We maintain a bank account with Board-approved signatories. Funds are used for organizational development, program implementation and administrative costs. Our Treasurer ensures transparency.

Sustainable Model

Sustainability is in our constitution (Article 11). We don't just do projects; we build lasting resilience.

Locally Led

We are based in Kigorobya Town Council. We are the community. No overhead to foreign offices.

Frequently asked questions

Clear information helps donors, partners and community members engage with confidence.

Where does RHUL work?

RHUL is based in Kigorobya Town Council, Kigorobya, Uganda, with a vision to serve communities in Western Uganda and beyond.

What are RHUL’s main areas of work?

Women’s empowerment, child protection and rights, community health, climate resilience and sustainable development are central to the organization’s work.

Partners can connect through the contact section to discuss funding, technical assistance, community programs, capacity building or other forms of collaboration.
